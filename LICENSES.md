`LICENSE-CODE` applies MIT to original research code. Neither relicenses
third-party material. Bundled Palumbo upstream code retains its MIT notice,
copyright (c) 2025 nilspalumbo, in each `sat_case/upstream/LICENSE` file.

from inclusion. See source provenance alongside the upstream files.

redistributed: the inherited licence chain from progress-measures-paper was not
established. Exact external retrieval is optional and explicit via
`setup_external.py`; it is required for native replay and is not a permission grant.

Photographic fixtures without comprehensive redistribution clearance, the full
third-party web essay, and opaque legacy pickles are excluded. Their historical
identities are not grants of rights or claims of public revalidation. Mathematical
results, original numerical arrays and original figures are distinct from those
excluded source assets.
