# Public evidence scope

The primary SAT study and its numerical inputs are distributed. Five historical
image-dependent supporting stages are not distributed because comprehensive
redistribution rights for their photographic inputs have not been established:
`inception_saved`, `construct_validity`, `geometry_failure`,
`geometry_bisection`, and `fixed_seam_saved`.

Opaque legacy pickles and the full third-party Goodfire web essay are also not
distributed. The margin replay excludes 17 pickle hash checks and labels their
byte counts historical metadata. The separate task-policy negative-score screen
checks two available manifest members and reports one additional unavailable
pickle byte-identity check. One unavailable historical diagnostic log,
`sat_renaming_launch_recovery/native_replay.log`, is not byte-revalidated; all
shipped scientific renaming arrays and numerical checks remain required. The
adapted renaming verifier is bound by the public package manifest, not asserted
byte-identical to its historical source.

The public replay runner therefore targets 18 stages, not 23. Its result is not
evidence that an omitted stage ran. The publisher's retained full-input replay is
distinct from what a reader can reproduce from this public package.

Native replay additionally requires three exact hash-pinned upstream source files
that are not redistributed because inherited permission is unresolved. Retrieve
them explicitly with `setup_external.py` into a new external directory, then pass
that directory to `reproduce.py --upstream-directory`. The runner validates the
external bytes and their disposable copies; it does not retrieve them implicitly.
Public retrieval is not a grant of redistribution rights.

`OMITTED_IMAGE_ASSETS.json` preserves file identities and historical source URLs
without photographic pixels. URLs do not constitute permission to redistribute.
Historical source URLs may cease to resolve. No automatic download is required
or attempted by the public runner.

Selected historical prose has presentation-only derivatives. Scientific arrays,
statistics and tests are not altered by that editorial cleanup. Historical
identities and public derivative identities must be distinguished in manifests;
the public derivative is not represented as the originally frozen document.
