The paper reports a bounded single-checkpoint SAT case: strong assignment ranking,
failed narrower threshold gates, an effective rank-34 state, retrospective basis
controls, exact final-cut readout accounting and semantic edit-response limits.
Locally fitted NMF recovers 32 native synthesis axes at cosine at least .99 but has
3.15% reconstruction error despite an available exact identity factorisation.
Its small matching improvement does not identify a uniquely preferred representation.
The Supplement retains mathematical calibrations, earlier pilots and source audits
with their distinct evidence scopes. Historical records and superseded diagnostics
are retained as provenance, not new protected confirmations.

The completed full Anthropic review and the documented minor corrections are in
the root review/provenance files. This anonymous manuscript is unrefereed; no
