# Saved-data repairs after the full-envelope manuscript review

These analyses answer four decision-changing findings from the naturally
completed Opus 5.5 manuscript review. They are exploratory diagnostics on retained
arrays. They make zero new transformer forwards and do not alter the protected
status of the earlier SAT-renaming execution.

## Components

- `action_menu_encoding_factorial.py` crosses all-slot/sparse-fit and
  selected-slot/dense-fit menus with one-hot and natural-binary edit encodings.
  It reproduces the original pilot before fitting the other seven fixed models.
- `readiness_semantic_baseline.py` compares the frozen structured readiness rule
  with the declared semantic-action-effect rule under the exact saved 2,000
  assessment-origin bootstrap.
- `clamp_pair_null.py` evaluates every unordered pair of 512 final-block units on
  the two source-matched positive endpoint cohorts, plus pairs within a factor 1.5
  of each source pair's activation-change RMS.
- `sat_information_audit.py` groups all legal edits by exact report and action,
  reproduces the archived lookup bounds, reports conflict prevalence over
  non-singleton classes, and refines published classes with complete truth tables
  to separate target ambiguity from report-specific loss.
- `renaming_threshold_audit.py` reports per-direction score distributions,
  exact pairwise AUROC, zero-false-positive sensitivity, nonzero origin values,
  and the detection-by-origin-restoration cross-tab.
- `direction_specific_clamp_null.py` ranks all 512 single units and all 130,816
  unordered pairs separately on each 32-endpoint construction direction. Its
  fully non-source pair comparator excludes both nominated units.

## Interpretive boundary

The information audit quantifies the collision as both a bank-level
target-specification and report-sufficiency result. The factorial shows dependence on action encoding rather than a general ordering
of bits and magnitudes. The readiness baseline isolates an increment beyond the
semantic action label but does not equalise supervision with the continuous native
score comparator. The threshold audit distinguishes source-threshold sensitivity
from score ranking. The direction-specific null establishes strong source-aligned
direct-logit attribution on the saved cohorts; it does not establish sufficiency,
unique upstream mediation or a natural activation-manifold intervention.

Every generated JSON file records inputs, settings, results and scope. The NPZ
files retain the paired bootstrap or enumeration arrays used in the reports.
