# Publication-stage source and contribution check

18 October 2026. This is a bounded intake/revision check, not a priority certificate or an independent literature review of every cited item.

## Decision-relevant sources

- [Palumbo et al., ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/palumbo25a.html) establishes the published source identity. The [version-2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.13594v2), sections 4.1–4.2 and Appendix D, already identifies 34 relevant units, positive contributions towards SAT and an approximate threshold interpretation. Those mechanisms are antecedents, not discoveries of this audit. The revised contribution table separates reproduction, additional quantification and new tests. The full text identifies its licence as CC BY 4.0; the code retains its own upstream terms.
- [Cammarata, Neural Networks Are Already Clean](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/), especially the sections distinguishing superposition meanings, separates feature-count claims from a proposed learning mechanism. Neither effective width nor a native-coordinate ranking advantage alone discriminates those accounts. The revised paper retains this limitation. The entire webpage is not redistributed merely because it is publicly readable.
- [Geiger et al., Causal Abstraction](https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html) is an acknowledged antecedent for intervention-dependent faithfulness, not evidence that the present finite report is a validated causal abstraction.

## Increment and limits

The release's contribution is a scoped empirical audit: fixed published labels, protected endpoint distinctions, exact finite edit obstruction, new same-checkpoint input-bank evaluation, and transparent readout accounting. It does not establish a universal representation theory, a new general abstraction theorem, or priority over all interpretability audits. The frozen new-bank results support input-distribution robustness only in their balanced, history-excluded source grammar. The failed short training feasibility attempts do not provide cross-checkpoint mechanism evidence.

The supplied review and archived scholarly source ledger provide additional citation context. Their existence is not a substitute for reading load-bearing arguments. The present check confirms the principal source-to-claim boundary; it does not claim a fresh full-text verification of every background reference or exhaustive search of all contemporary public repositories.
