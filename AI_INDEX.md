Title: Assignment-Aligned Effective States in a 2-SAT Transformer.
Version: 4.8.0. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23454637 (reserved; availability unverified).

Start with `paper.md`, then `supplement.md`, `claim_evidence_map.json` and
`PUBLIC_PACKAGE_SCOPE.md`. The study separates semantic assignment labels,
native-coordinate threshold events, learned-basis prediction, numerical rank,
and readout sufficiency. None is interchangeable with causal semantic recovery.

`reproduce.py` targets 18 historical stages over redistributed inputs and exact
external dependencies. Native replay additionally requires exact externally
retrieved architecture sources listed in `EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCIES.json`; the
explicit setup command does not grant redistribution rights. Five image-dependent
stages are unavailable. `prospective/README.md` describes the fresh source-style
bank and portable native evaluation. Consult `ASSURANCE.md` before attributing
verification status. Failed or unavailable checks are not successes.

Historical frozen files remain historical. The pinned presentation-derivative
map binds edited prose separately. The margin replay excludes 17 unavailable
pickle-byte checks; its remaining arithmetic and shipped-input checks are
separately reported. Do not load opaque model pickles; none are distributed.
